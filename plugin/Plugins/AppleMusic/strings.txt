PLUGIN_APPLEMUSIC_NAME
	EN	Apple Music

PLUGIN_APPLEMUSIC_DESC
	EN	Stream your Apple Music subscription (unofficial). Supply a Widevine CDM; the helper and ffmpeg download automatically.

PLUGIN_APPLEMUSIC_CDM_DESC
	EN	Point at your own Widevine L3 CDM. Either a single device.wvd file, or a client_id.bin + private_key.pem pair. Nothing is bundled. Required for catalog playback.

PLUGIN_APPLEMUSIC_FFMPEG
	EN	ffmpeg

PLUGIN_APPLEMUSIC_FFMPEG_DESC
	EN	The helper needs ffmpeg 6.1 or newer (older builds cannot decrypt Apple's streams). "Download automatically" fetches a verified static build for this platform.
